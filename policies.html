Nrityarang Event Booking

About us

This website is operated for Nrityarang's annual cultural performance. We provide an online booking platform for attendees to view available seat layouts and purchase entrance tickets for our live dance event.

1. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancellations by the User

All ticket sales for Nrityarang Event are final. We do not offer cancellations, refunds, or exchanges once a ticket has been purchased and the payment has been successfully processed.

Cancellations by the Organizer

In the rare event that Nrityarang Event is cancelled or postponed by the organizers due to unforeseen circumstances, buyers will be notified using the email address or phone number provided during booking.

  • If the event is cancelled, a full refund will be initiated to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
  • If the event is postponed, the ticket will remain valid for the new date, or the buyer may request a refund within the specified period mentioned in the postponement notice.

Failed Transactions

If the payment amount is deducted from your bank account but the ticket booking is not completed successfully, the amount will be refunded to the original payment method according to the applicable payment gateway processing timelines.

2. Terms & Conditions

* Every attendee occupying a seat must hold a valid digital confirmation ticket.

* Seats are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis upon successful payment confirmation.

* Attendees must arrive at the venue at least 15 to 20 minutes prior to the scheduled start time.

* The event organizers reserve the right of admission and may refuse entry in compliance with venue safety and security protocols.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

* Ticket Bookings: All ticket bookings made through this platform are final, strictly non-cancellable, and non-refundable once confirmed.

* Event Cancellation: In the rare event that the performance is canceled or postponed due to unforeseen or unavoidable circumstances, a full refund will be processed to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days.

* Failed / Duplicate Transactions: If funds are debited from your bank account or UPI app but no ticket confirmation is generated due to a network or technical failure, the debited amount will be automatically refunded to your original payment source within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Shipping & Delivery Policy

As this portal operates exclusively for live event ticketing, no physical goods or printed paper tickets are shipped or delivered via courier. Your digital seat confirmation and ticket details are delivered immediately on-screen upon successful transaction completion, and a copy is sent via WhatsApp / SMS / Email to the contact details provided during checkout.

5. Pricing & Payment

All ticket prices are displayed in Indian Rupees (INR). Payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your credit card, debit card, or other sensitive payment credentials on our website.

6. Privacy Policy

Data Collection

When you book a ticket through our website, we may collect basic personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and booking details.

Use of Information

The information collected is used to process your booking, provide ticket confirmation, communicate important booking information, and notify you about changes or updates related to the event.

Data Protection

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party marketing services. Your details are used solely for admission verification and event-related communication.

Payment Security

Payment information is processed securely by Razorpay. We do not directly store your credit card, debit card, UPI credentials, or banking passwords on our website.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational precautions to protect the personal information collected through our website from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.
